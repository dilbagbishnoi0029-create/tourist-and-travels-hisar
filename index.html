Who we are

From our home in Hisar, we plan journeys the way we would plan them for family — slowly, thoughtfully, and with room for the unexpected. From the backwaters of Kerala to the high passes of Ladakh, every itinerary is built around how you like to travel.

No call centres. No copy-paste tours. Just a small team of travellers, local partners, and one shared belief: the best trips leave you with stories, not a checklist.
